Application forms

Every link opens the form from the official government website, so you always get the current version. Print copies before the drive day.

Print in bulk

PMSBY, PMJJBY and the Jan Dhan form are needed most. Print Gujarati and Hindi copies. Most mess and housekeeping staff read one of the two.

Where forms go

Insurance, APY, Jan Dhan and Sukanya forms go to the worker's own bank branch or post office. Ujjwala forms go to any LPG distributor. Ration and widow pension forms go to the Mamlatdar office.

No paper form

e-Shram, PM-SYM, Ayushman card, PMAY, PMMVY, JSY and the construction workers' card are done online, at a CSC, or by the Anganwadi or ASHA worker. Use the portal links instead.

The 10-minute conversation

  1. Greet in their language. Hindi or Gujarati first. Say who you are: a student volunteer, not from their employer or contractor.
  2. Ask before you explain. Go through the questions on the "Check a worker" tab. Don't ask for Aadhaar numbers, only whether they have one.
  3. Start with e-Shram. It is free and opens the door to the rest. Then the two insurance covers, which together cost ₹456 a year.
  4. Use the one-line pitch on each result card. Keep it to 2–3 schemes per worker. Too many at once confuses.
  5. Register on the spot where the worker has their Aadhaar-linked phone. The OTP goes to their phone and they read it out themselves.
  6. Hand over the printed form for bank, LPG, ration or pension schemes. Help fill it, and tell them where to submit it.
  7. Tap "Done, next worker" to add them to today's tally. Nothing about them is saved.

Questions workers ask

"Do I have to pay anything?"

e-Shram, Jan Dhan, Ayushman card and ration card are free. PMSBY is ₹20 a year and PMJJBY ₹436 a year, cut from their bank account. Nobody at this drive takes cash. If anyone asks for a fee, tell a coordinator.

"Will my contractor or employer know?"

No. These are their own registrations with the government. They don't change their job, salary or PF.

"PF is cut from my salary. Can I still join?"

EPF/ESIC members cannot join e-Shram or PM-SYM. They can still take PMSBY, PMJJBY and Ayushman (if eligible). Check the payslip if they are unsure.

"What if I go back to my village?"

The e-Shram number works anywhere in India. With One Nation One Ration Card, their ration card works at any ration shop in any state.

"Is my Aadhaar safe?"

We don't write down or photocopy anything. The OTP is read only from their own phone, on the government site. Tell them never to share an OTP over a phone call.

"Why do I need a nominee?"

For the insurance and pension schemes, the nominee is who gets the money. Ask them to give a family member's name and age.

"When is the insurance renewed?"

PMJJBY and PMSBY run June to May. The amount is cut around 31 May, so they should keep that much in the account.
